STATEMENTS

LatinQuarter/Textbook.lean states Theorems A and B with the definitions of
Section 1 of arXiv:2610.18199, written without the package's own definitions:
Latin squares with each symbol exactly once in every row and column, filled
cells as (row, column, symbol) triples, completions counted with Nat.card,
critical sets defined by emptying one filled cell, and scs(n) as the minimum
of scs(L) over all Latin squares L. Audit.lean prints these definitions and
the theorem statements.
